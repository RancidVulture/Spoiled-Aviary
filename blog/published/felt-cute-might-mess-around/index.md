My original idea for a Fortnite map was to celebrate the 100th anniversary of the silent film, "Metropolis," somehow. Maybe silent film ~~fans~~ purists and film ~~snobs~~ historians would cringe and cry blasphemy at the thought of Metropolis collaborating with Fortnite, but as the grandfather of every science-fiction film, I can't think of a more dystopian science-fiction way to celebrate 100 years of the film being released than to combine the now public domain intellectual property with one of the biggest most profitable video games ever made.

But then I realized there was **money** to be made. Instead of toiling to produce an ultimately extremely niche map that hardly any Fortnite players would understand the reference material for, why not go more generic and make a tycoon map? The logic is simple enough and can even be done on consoles within Fortnite's in-game creative mode. You just can't import custom assets, obviously. Specific, different in-game items within the creative mode have different pieces or bits of programming logic attached to them with various adjustable settings for them in a handful of menus. So within a matter of minutes you can have the basic bones of a tycoon map up and running; collect in-game currency every second that ticks by, redeem said currency to unlock this building or this space or this item or weapon, etc.

But then I realized I needed to make my tycoon map **unique** and **stand out**. That's when an ethereal whisper came to me and fluttered by my ears, " . . . *use your hand-drawn artwork as paintings players can unlock in an Art Gallery Tycoon map*." I was inspired. I thought I had my idea and I was off to the races in my Mach 5. Hell yea brudder.

![screenshot-2026-09-26-005627-mwy8tswt-3g8fi.webp](/blog/images/screenshot-2026-09-26-005627-mwy8tswt-3g8fi.webp "A random example of some of my "art." I thought seeing images such as these in a Fortnite map could become a meme, and boost the map's popularity.")

"*Not so fast*," either my conscious or my ego then says, "*let's say you do make some money at creating Fortnite maps, do you really want to be profiting off children's gambling and video game addiction issues??*"

My answer was a resounding, "No." So I'm going back to the drawing board a third time. Probably not a tycoon map, not some obscure silent film from literally 100 years ago-appreciation map, maybe just something for fun, something sort of in the middle of these two extremes. I guess I'm still trying to decide but this was just a glimpse into my thought processes during this creative project I hope to see through to the end.

Happy Thanksgiving and all that jazz. Hope everyone is doing well and holding on OK. I really do appreciate everyone as always. Thanks, everyone.
